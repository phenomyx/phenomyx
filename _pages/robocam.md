## RoboCam

<img src="/assets/img/robocam.jpg" alt="RoboCam" style="max-width: 80%; display: block; margin: auto;">



<section class="product-summary">
  <p>
    <strong>RoboCam</strong> is a robotic laboratory imaging platform for
    time-lapse imaging of samples in multiwell plate formats.
  </p>

  <p>
    It is designed for repeated, long-term imaging of small biological samples,
    including embryos, motile organisms, and other specimens typically cultured
    in multiwell plates.
  </p>

  <h3>Key features</h3>

  <ul>
    <li>Robotic motion in X, Y and Z dimensions</li>
    <li>Versatile lens options for different magnifications and plate formats</li>
    <li>Experiment setup via touchscreen, mouse, or keyboard</li>
    <li>Adjustable experiment settings, including video duration, recording frequency, and total experiment duration</li>
    <li>Adjustable camera settings, including gain, exposure, and frame rate</li>
    <li>Integrated vibration isolation in the instrument feet</li>
    <li>Multiwell plate humidification chamber to reduce evaporation</li>
  </ul>

    We also develop computer vision and AI software for biological image analysis.
    Whether you need to identify, measure, track, or count biological samples,
    we may be able to help.
  </p>
